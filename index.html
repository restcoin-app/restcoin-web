How It Works

Screen-Off Focus Tracking

Automatically tracks your screen-off duration during work, study, or rest—helping you reduce digital fatigue and reclaim your attention.

Check in at designated quiet zones, libraries, cafes, or partner venues to boost your focus progress with location-based multipliers.

Gamified Habit Rewards

Earn non-transferable in-app virtual points for completing daily focus milestones and 60-minute marathon sessions.
